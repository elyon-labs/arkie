# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** April 29, 2026

## Overview

This application (“the App”) is an open-source RCON client released under the MIT License. The App is designed to allow users to connect directly to their own game servers using an IP address and port, and to issue RCON commands of their choosing.

We take privacy seriously and have intentionally designed the App to collect **no personal data** and transmit **no information** to any developer-controlled or third-party servers.

## Information We Collect

The App does **not** collect, log, transmit, or process any of the following:

- Personal information  
- Usage analytics  
- Telemetry or crash reports  
- IP addresses other than those you explicitly enter to connect to your own server  

If you save a server connection in the App, the server name, address, port, and RCON password are stored locally on your device so the App can reconnect to that server later. This information is not uploaded, synchronized, or sent anywhere except to the configured server when you initiate a connection.

## Network Communication

- The App connects **only** to servers that you explicitly configure (via IP address and port).
- All network traffic is initiated by the user and directed exclusively to their own server.
- The App does **not** communicate with any developer-controlled, third-party, or analytics servers.

## Data Storage

- Any configuration data, including saved server connections and saved RCON passwords, is stored locally on your device.
- No data is synchronized, uploaded, or backed up by the App itself.

## Third-Party Services

The App does **not** integrate with or use any third-party services that collect data.

## Open Source Transparency

The App is fully open-sourced under the MIT License. Users are free to inspect the source code to verify its behavior and confirm that no data collection or transmission occurs beyond what is described here.

## Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

## Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy changes, the updated version will be made available alongside the App’s source code. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

## Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the App’s behavior, please contact the project maintainer via the repository’s issue tracker.
